Why BitMine Lost $6.313B in FY2026: The 10-K Breakdown — Unrealized ETH Loss, 97% Staking Revenue, No Debt

Written by Crypto Desk · Last verified: 2026-11-12 05:15 UTC

On November 10–11, 2026, BitMine Immersion Technologies (BMNR) reported a net loss of $6.313 billion in its 10-K for the fiscal year ended August 31, 2026 (The BlockBeats relay of the SEC filing, 2026-11-11 01:10:37 UTC; The Block and Business Insider round the same filing to $6.3B, OKX Orbit's analysis to $6.32B) — against a $349M profit in the prior fiscal year. The loss is almost entirely a paper mark: ~$6.229B of unrealized fair-value losses on its ETH stack, while the company sold zero coins and earned $124.8M (97% of it from staking) in the same year.

The Mechanism: Why a Company That Sold Zero Coins "Lost" $6.3B

This is the long-tail part — it holds after the burst ends, and it answers the question every reader arrives with: did BitMine blow up?

  • Fair-value accounting does the work. Digital assets are measured at fair value each period, changes recognized in earnings. The 10-K: "Because our digital assets are measured at fair value, with changes in fair value recognized in earnings, changes in ETH prices are expected to continue to cause significant volatility in our reported results, stockholders' equity and financial condition." [BI, quoting the filing]
  • The driver is one number: the 44% ETH decline. Ether fell ~44% across FY2026, to near $2,466 by Aug 31 [BI; The Block headline; CMC guest post, all 11-11]. On a ~$19.9B cost basis that books ~$6.229B of unrealized loss [BlockBeats relay].
  • The business itself grew. Revenue $6.1M → $124.8M (beat the $119.8M consensus); staking = $121.4M = 97% [BI; BlockBeats $125M/$121M — dual caliber]. The loss is the mark, not the operations.
  • The balance sheet has no leverage to break. Per OKX Orbit: total assets $15.43B ($14.6B = the 5.9M ETH, 94.6%), liabilities $33.7M = 0.22% [pending verification #2] — a falling ETH price shrinks NAV but cannot by itself trigger a liquidation.
  • The funding side is equity. BitMine sold 395.1M common shares for $13.2B gross over the fiscal year [pending verification #2]; management has bought back 20.8M shares at $17 avg, with $3.6B left of a $4B buyback plan [BI].

Cross-foot self-check (numbers that must add up): ETH share of assets: $14.6B / $15.43B = 94.6% ✓ (matches OKX's stated share). Staking share of revenue: $121.4M / $124.8M = 97.3% ✓ (matches the 97% figure). Cost basis − end-of-period fair value: $19.948B − $14.559B = $5.389B year-end mark-down — smaller than the full-year $6.229B unrealized loss, consistent with ETH trading above average cost during the year before falling below by Aug 31 (internal-consistency note).

FAQ

Did BitMine sell ETH to realize the loss? No — unrealized fair-value movement; zero coins sold [BI].

$6.313B, $6.3B, or $6.32B — which is right? Same 10-K, three roundings: $6.313B (BlockBeats relay), $6.3B (The Block, BI), $6.32B (OKX, written as 63.2×10⁸). We write $6.313B and note the dual caliber.

Could this become a liquidity crisis? Not on the balance sheet as filed (0.22% debt ratio [pending verification #2]); the fixed cost is the 9.50% preferred dividend, which dollar staking yield must keep covering [10-K via BI].

What if ETH recovers? The mark reverses through earnings in the opposite direction — the same 10-K sentence produced a +$349M profit year in FY2025.

When does the buying stop? At 5% of supply — ~40,000 ETH from the 11-08 count, about four weeks at the recent pace [BI].
