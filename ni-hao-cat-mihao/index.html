What Is mihao

Per the BingX listing description (11-11): a community meme token issued on Solana that combines the Chinese greeting "ni hao" (hello), a cat image, and the "Very Chinese Time" online culture. Official site: mihao.fun; X: @iamnihaocat (as listed on CoinGecko). Two structural facts from the 11-11 KuCoin/Odaily report: developers locked 5% of supply for one year, and prominent trader Ansem publicly called the token (avatar-community shareability + Chinese-culture angle, framed as his cycle's "pure meme" alongside his ANSEM token) — an endorsement, not a recommendation. It is a meme: no protocol, no revenue, price = attention.
