How a Company Gets on This List (The Page-Living Mechanism)

  • Public companies: a treasury announcement or an 8-K / 10-K disclosing a material crypto position adds a row — holdings, % of supply and the stated strategy (buy cadence, staking, cap) come straight from the filing.
  • On-chain companies: a verifiable on-chain position (wallet balance, vault TVL) with a public entity behind it adds a row, with the chain/data source cited.
  • Weekly refresh: BitMine reports holdings weekly — the last verified point is 2026-11-08 (published 11-11). Each new weekly update moves the numbers on this page and on the cap countdown page.
  • Why it matters: corporate treasuries are a standing marginal-buyer category. Every 8-K in this space is a fresh data point, and this page is where the running total lives.

FAQ

Is August Digital an ETH company? No — it is a USDC vault curator on Monad. It is listed here as the contrast case from the same news week; the ETH column shows what it actually holds (nothing — the asset is USDC).

Where do the BitMine numbers come from? The Nov 9 holdings update (6,070,092 ETH = 4.97%), relayed by The BlockBeats 11-11 and confirmed by Business Insider 11-11; the 5% cap and ~40,000 ETH headroom from Business Insider / The CC Press. Full source list on the BitMine page.

How many ETH treasury companies are there? Three lines on this list today: two true ETH treasuries (BitMine, SharpLink) and one crypto-treasury contrast (August Digital). The list grows by disclosure, not by curation.

Is SharpLink's $1.08B loss comparable to BitMine's $6.313B? No — different company, different period, different accounting snapshot: SharpLink's $1.08B is H1-2026 (six months ended 2026-06-30, filed 08-07, mostly non-cash marks), BitMine's $6.313B is the full FY2026 10-K. See the F6 note in the table and the BitMine loss page.
