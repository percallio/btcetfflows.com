Why the Fee Column Matters (GBTC's 1.50%)

  • The spread is 1.35 points a year. GBTC's 1.50% fee vs 0.14–0.25% for the other eleven tickers (MSBT lowest at 0.14%, BTC 0.15%, EZBC 0.19%, BITB/HODL 0.20%) is the single largest structural cost difference inside the complex.
  • That spread is the structural outflow story. Since the 0.25% spot-ETF alternatives began trading in January 2024, GBTC has been a net source of outflows — its cumulative is −US$28.1B since launch (Farside, per our GBTC page) against the complex's cumulative of +US$55.2B. The −US$100.1M in the window above (three outflow sessions, none positive) is the same story at week scale.
  • The two Grayscale lines are different products. GBTC (1.50%) is the legacy trust; BTC (0.15%) is Grayscale's second, low-fee product — they can and do flow in different directions on the same day (in the window above: GBTC −100.1, BTC −52.6).
