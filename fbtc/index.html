FBTC is the Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (Nasdaq, 0.25% fee), the complex's #2 fund by cumulative inflows since launch (+$10,238M, Farside — IBIT +$64,810M) and the window's only large fund to finish net positive: +$56.3M against a −$1,877.5M market, driven by the 11-04 single-day inflow of +$236.7M, the window's largest single-fund inflow and the reason 11-04 is its only positive market session; the 11-12 session is pending at pull and excluded.

FBTC was the only large fund to end the eight sessions 11-02 → 11-11 (2026) net positive: +$56.3M, against a market net of −$1,877.5M [Farside fund matrix, pulled 2026-11-12]. Positive on four of the eight sessions, with one day doing most of the work: 11-04, +$236.7M — the largest single-fund inflow of the whole window and the reason that day is its only positive market session.

What FBTC Is

  • Product: Fidelity's spot bitcoin ETF — an open-end fund that holds bitcoin, listed on Nasdaq, ticker FBTC, launched February 2024 (the second month of the spot-ETF wave that started in January 2024).
  • Fee: 0.25% — the Farside fee row puts FBTC at 0.25%, the same as IBIT and well under GBTC's 1.50%.
  • Sponsor/custody: Fidelity is the fund's sponsor and is also the custodian of the bitcoin it holds — one of the reasons the fund is positioned as the "bank-grade" option in the complex.

Scale Positioning

By cumulative inflows since launch, FBTC is #2 in the complex: +$10,238M (Farside cumulative row, as reported on the site's daily flows page) — about 18.6% of the market's +$55,170.6M cumulative net inflows (Farside cumulative chart, latest point 11-11), and a distant second to IBIT's +$64,810M. Fund-level AUM (dollar size today, not cumulative flows) is pending a satsintel/DefiLlama pull and is marked pending rather than estimated.
