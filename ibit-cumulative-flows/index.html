Why Cumulative Flows ≠ AUM

The US spot BTC ETF complex's AUM was US$93.70B on 2026-11-12 — US$38.5B above this cumulative figure. That gap is not missing flows: it is price revaluation (plus other valuation effects) on the bitcoin the funds already hold. AUM moves with both money in/out and the BTC price; cumulative flow moves only with money in/out. The dual-factor breakdown, cross-footed to 93,700.0, is on the AUM page.

FAQ

Is this IBIT's cumulative? No — this is the all-fund complex. Farside's cumulative chart does not publish single-fund cumulative; IBIT's own cumulative (+US$64.8B, largest single fund) is on our AUM page, and GBTC's (−US$28.1B since launch, Farside) is on the GBTC page.

When was the peak? In-month during October 2025, at US$62,735.9M cumulative. No point since has exceeded it; the current drawdown is US$7,565.3M.

How fresh is the data? The last point is 2026-11-11 (the 11-12 session was not yet reported on Farside at the 11-12 pull). This page refreshes with the daily pull.

What Is Not Included

This is the all-fund cumulative series: it does not include (a) any single-fund cumulative — Farside's chart provides the complex total only (IBIT +US$64.8B and GBTC −US$28.1B live on their pages: AUM / GBTC); (b) AUM — flows only, the ~US$38.5B revaluation gap is the AUM page's subject; (c) the full 729-point daily series — the table is month-end samples of it (Feb 2024 – Oct 2026, 33 complete months); (d) Nov 2026 and Jan 2024 as table rows — both are partial months, covered in the milestones instead.
