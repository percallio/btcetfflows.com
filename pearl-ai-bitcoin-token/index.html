Pearl (PEARL): The "AI Version of Bitcoin" — What It Actually Is

Written by Crypto Desk · Last verified: 2026-11-14 (data pulled 2026-11-14 06:15 UTC)

Answer (as of 2026-11-14 06:15 UTC):

Pearl is a proof-of-useful-work Layer-1 protocol whose mining is produced natively from AI computations — its pitch is a new implementation of GPU matrix-multiplication that lets GPUs emit proof-of-work as a side-effect of AI training (CoinGecko listing, pearlresearch.ai). The "AI version of Bitcoin" label came from Token2049, where Pearl was the most-mentioned project (KuCoin flash, 11-14, syndicated across HTX). On 11-14 it traded at $1.55, +18.5% over 24h but -12.4% over 7 days, $16.7M volume, $583.5M market cap, rank 91 — and 37% below its $2.46 all-time high set 2026-10-21 (CoinGecko API, 11-14 06:15 UTC); it sat #2 on the CoinGecko trending list in the 11-14 05:46 UTC snapshot. Treat this as an early-stage page: the token has only ~6 weeks of price history from its high, and the Token2049 buzz is a conference-attention event, not a protocol milestone.

The interesting question this page exists for: is "mine from your AI training run" a real consensus mechanism, or a relabel of proof-of-work? The listing data says what it is; the risks section says what could be wrong.

What Pearl is (per its own listing)

  • Mechanism: a Proof-of-Useful-Work (PUIW) L1. The core is "a new and efficient implementation of the core GPU operation (matrix-multiplication), allowing GPUs to implement proof-of-work as a side-effect of AI training" (CoinGecko description, from the project listing). In plain terms: if the claim holds, an AI training cluster gets to mine Pearl while doing the training it was going to do anyway — the hashwork is a byproduct, not dead compute.
  • Lineage signals: CoinGecko categories list it under Layer 1, Proof of Work, Artificial Intelligence, and Near Protocol Ecosystem — the Near association is worth verifying against the project's own docs before quoting it as a hard dependency (see awaiting verification).
  • The buzz: "most-mentioned project at Token2049" is a conference-attention fact (11-14 flash coverage), not a protocol metric — it explains the +18.5% day and the #2 trending position, and it will explain the reversal when the next conference cycle starts.
