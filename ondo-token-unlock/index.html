What an unlock of that size actually means

  • 19.4% of max supply in one release (January 2026) is a structural supply event: even if early backers sell only a fraction, the sellable float jumps by double digits. The price path since — including the -46% year-over-year figure in the 11-13 coverage — is the market digesting that overhang alongside the broader RWA sector's cooling.
  • "Unlock day" ≠ "sell day": tokens vest to teams, investors and ecosystems on schedule; actual selling depends on holder cost basis, lockups on top of vesting, and whether the protocol offers buyback/burn or staking sinks. The date is known, the flow is not — which is exactly why the window (not the day) gets priced.
  • For a $1.6B mcap token, a 1.94B-token release at the current ~$0.33 is worth on the order of ~$640M of newly releasable supply if it repeats — the single biggest overhang question in the ONDO thesis right now.

Why ONDO is trending (11-14)

The 11-13 CoinMarketCap community piece framed it as: a top-50 RWA token at $0.35, 46% below its year-ago level, with a major unlock approaching — a "known supply event + weak price" combination that reliably pulls search traffic. Context from this site's RWA map: ONDO's product side (Ondo Global Markets, MAG7X) keeps shipping while the token price grinds lower — product momentum and token price are not the same variable, and the unlock schedule is the bridge between them.
