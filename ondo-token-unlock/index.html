11-15 update — volume up while price fell

Three sessions of the same trade, measured: $0.3336 (11-14) → $0.3016 (11-15) is -9.6% in a day, on $152.9M of volume versus $92.2M the prior day. Rising volume into a falling price is the classic distribution signature — sellers are finding bids, and the bids are being filled. It is also how a supply-overhang token behaves when the market turns risk-off: BTC dropped 3.1% to $64,812 on the same snapshot, and the trending list's top five were BTC / HYPE / NEAR / ONDO / SOL, i.e. capital sitting in majors and one high-attention RWA name.

What this does not change: the 2027-01-18 unlock date, the ~1.94B-token January 2026 precedent, or the fact that no official Ondo Foundation confirmation has been published on our pulls. A weaker price going into an unlock window is the textbook setup people search for — which is why this page exists — but the page will not translate price action into a forecast.

12 hours later — the volume retreat

The section above called the 11-15 move "distribution": rising volume into a falling price. That reading has a testable follow-through — if sellers were being absorbed, volume should stay high; if the flow was a one-session event, volume should collapse once the mark stops falling. Measured at 11-15 17:50 UTC:

  • Price stabilised: $0.3016 → $0.3040 (+0.8%), i.e. the -8.9% session stopped falling.
  • Volume retreated 17%: $152.9M → $127.6M, still elevated versus the 11-14 $92.2M but below the distribution peak.
  • Attention left the top-15: #4 in the morning snapshot, outside the top-15 by evening.

Read together: a one-session distribution into a market-wide drawdown, then quiet — not an ongoing bleed. That is the more benign of the two readings, and it is only available because the earlier version of this section committed to a mechanism. The unlock facts are unchanged: next scheduled release 2027-01-18, prior release ~1.94B ONDO (~19.4% of max supply), still pending an official Ondo Foundation confirmation.

What an unlock of that size actually means

  • 19.4% of max supply in one release (January 2026) is a structural supply event: even if early backers sell only a fraction, the sellable float jumps by double digits. The price path since — including the -46% year-over-year figure in the 11-13 coverage — is the market digesting that overhang alongside the broader RWA sector's cooling.
  • "Unlock day" ≠ "sell day": tokens vest to teams, investors and ecosystems on schedule; actual selling depends on holder cost basis, lockups on top of vesting, and whether the protocol offers buyback/burn or staking sinks. The date is known, the flow is not — which is exactly why the window (not the day) gets priced.
  • For a $1.6B mcap token, a 1.94B-token release at the current ~$0.33 is worth on the order of ~$640M of newly releasable supply if it repeats — the single biggest overhang question in the ONDO thesis right now.
