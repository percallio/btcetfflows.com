Why Two Numbers (Caliber, Explained for the Reader)

  1. DefiLlama = one vault, one chain, one method. The adapter measures AUSD via the Monad vault's getTotalAssets() — it is a custody-asset count for earnAUSD only [DefiLlama methodology].
  2. TradingStrategy = the curator's visible book. It aggregates every vault it can see (six, across AUSD/USDC denoms, Monad/Aptos, Upshift host) and explicitly flags that more may exist outside supported chains [TradingStrategy].
  3. The overlap is the anchor: $21.71M (DefiLlama) vs $21.73M (TradingStrategy's main-vault row) — a $20K difference between two independent counters of the same on-chain function, which is why this page carries a dual-source tag on the core asset.
  4. What neither covers: vaults outside both trackers, and any yield accrued but not yet marked. When the two counters drift meaningfully (>1%), that is the signal something structural moved — re-pull both and annotate.
  5. Peak discrepancy (not a contradiction): TradingStrategy shows the main vault's peak at $80.71M while DefiLlama's tracked history peaks at $31.47M — DefiLlama's tracking starts 2026-09-03, TradingStrategy's vault age is 1.0y, so the $80.71M predates DefiLlama's window [pending verification #2: which instrument produced the $80.71M reading — the vault's own history vs a related AUSD instrument — cannot be settled from the two sources alone].
