The campaign is not one offer with one rate. It is three separate pools with different assets, different windows and different qualification rules — the most common misreading of this campaign is treating the headline "up to 15%" as a single rate you can get by holding anything.

How a Distribution Actually Works

  1. Open the qualifying position inside the pool window. Each pool has its own start date — USD1 on 11-10 00:00 UTC, USDe on 11-11, KGST on 11-12 08:00 UTC. Being early to a pool that has not started yet earns nothing; being late to one that has closed the same.
  2. Keep it there through the qualifying period. For USDe that is explicitly 24 hours. For the others the announcement defines position type and cap rather than a minimum hold, but the payout logic still depends on you holding during the accrual window.
  3. Wait for the payout date. Distribution dates are 11-20, 11-27 and 12-04. There is no distribution on 11-13 — if you were expecting an early payout, that is where the confusion comes from.
  4. Do not look for a claim button. No claim step appears in the announcement. Rewards are distributed, not redeemed. This is the single most common wasted hour on this campaign.
  5. Compare the realised rate against the headline. After the first payout you can compute what you actually received and see which tier your balance landed in — that is the only reliable way to convert an "up to" figure into a real number.

Common Mistakes

  1. Treating "up to 15%" as a flat 15%. It is the KGST Flexible ceiling, composed of 10% bonus tier plus 5% real-time APR, on a subscription band of 1–1,500,000 KGST. Mixing it with the USD1 or USDe rate produces a number that exists on no document.
  2. Hunting for a claim button. Nothing in the announcement requires a claim. Time spent looking for one is time not spent on eligibility.
  3. Expecting a payout on 11-13. The distribution dates are 11-20 / 11-27 / 12-04. The gap between campaign start (11-10) and first payout (11-20) is normal, not a delay.
  4. Holding in the wrong account type. A USD1 balance in a product the announcement does not list is invisible to the campaign, however long it sits there.
  5. Mixing up this campaign with Launchpool or HODLer airdrops. Those are separate, permanently-running product lines with their own mechanics. They are excluded from this calendar on purpose — a row here means a dated campaign, not an exchange feature.
  6. Assuming the campaign carries over. Edition-one and edition-two appreciation campaigns are closed and are not re-listed. Nothing in the announcement extends this edition beyond 12-11 (the last pool close, KGST).

FAQ

Is there anything else claimable on Binance right now? As of the 2026-11-12 check, one campaign is on this calendar — the 3rd Community Appreciation. Fee-tier promos and Launchpool events are exchange product lines, not calendar rows (see What Is Not Included).

Do I need to claim anything? No claim step is in the announcement. Rewards distribute on the fixed dates (11-20 / 11-27 / 12-04) if you held or subscribed the qualifying asset in the qualifying account type during that pool's window.

What are the three distribution dates? 2026-11-20, 2026-11-27 and 2026-12-04. There is no distribution on 11-13. The 11-20 first payout is confirmed by Binance investor relations.

Which assets qualify? Three pools — USD1 (up to 10% APR), USDe (up to 10%, 5.25% base + up to 4.75% bonus, balances up to 100,000 USDe) and KGST Flexible (up to 15%, 10% bonus tier + 5% real-time APR, 1–1,500,000 KGST).

Do I need KYC? Yes — the announcement requires a KYC-complete account. USD1 must sit in Spot / Margin / Futures / Funding / Earn; USDe requires at least 0.01 USDe held for 24 hours; KGST requires a Flexible subscription.

What does "up to 10% APR" actually mean? A ceiling, not a flat rate. Each pool is tiered as a base rate plus a bonus component, so the realised rate depends on which tier your balance falls into. The full tier schedule is not printed in the announcement text and remains pending verification.

What if Binance amends a date? The amendment gets re-verified against the official text and the row is updated with the new date and a dated note.

What Is Not Included
