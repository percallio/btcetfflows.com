Binance's third community appreciation campaign is live — and it is not a classic claim-style airdrop but a hold-to-earn rewards campaign: hold or subscribe qualifying assets (USD1, USDe, KGST) on Binance over fixed windows, and the exchange distributes rewards to you on three scheduled dates (2026-11-20, 11-27, 12-04). How This Campaign Works (Hold-to-Earn, Not Claim)

The official announcement frames it as a rewards campaign across three stablecoin products, each with its own window, yield and pool [Binance announcement, original text]:

  • Eligibility = holding or subscribing, not tasks: USD1 held in Spot / Margin / Futures / Funding / Earn accounts; USDe ≥0.01 held 24h; KGST via a Flexible product subscription.
  • Rewards accrue over the window and pay out on fixed dates: 2026-11-20, 2026-11-27 and 2026-12-04 — and explicitly no distribution on 2026-11-13 [announcement].
  • "Up to X% APR" is a ceiling: each pool pairs a base with a tiered bonus; the max (10%/10%/15%) applies to top-tier holdings [announcement; the tier table itself is not printed — pending verification].
  • Edition three. The announcement calls it the third community appreciation campaign — editions one and two already paid out; this page covers the 2026-11 edition only.

Why "hold-to-earn" matters: you park a stablecoin balance (or a Flexible subscription) to earn an exchange-distributed reward; your principal is the stablecoin product itself, and the yield is a bonus on top, paid in the pool's asset (the USD1 pool pays WLFI and USD1).

How to Join (Step by Step)

Pool A — USD1 (open since 11-10, runs to 12-04):

  1. KYC-complete account needed; buy USD1 on spot or convert from a stablecoin balance.
  2. Hold USD1 in any of: Spot, Margin, Futures, Funding or Earn. The all-holdings portion is uncapped per user — bigger qualifying balance, bigger share, up to the 10% ceiling [announcement].
  3. If you are new to USD1 (or adding on top of an existing balance): the separate 2.5M USD1 pool covers first-time users and incremental holdings, capped at your first 50,000 new/incremental USD1 [announcement].
  4. Rewards land in your account on schedule at 11-20 / 11-27 / 12-04 — no claim step in the announcement.

Pool B — USDe (open since 11-11, runs to 11-30 23:59):

  1. Hold at least 0.01 USDe on Binance for 24 hours — the 24h is the qualifying condition, not a minimum [announcement].

Pool C — KGST Flexible (opens 11-12 08:00 UTC, runs to 12-11 23:59):

  1. Subscribe to a KGST Flexible product on Binance — Flexible = redeemable on demand; the campaign qualifies this line specifically [announcement].

  3. Yield: up to 15% APR = 10% bonus tier + 5% real-time APR [announcement]. Distributions on the same three dates.

Risks and Fine Print

  • The principal is the stablecoin. USD1/USDe/KGST each carry their own peg, issuer and redemption mechanics — a peg wobble during the window costs more than the APR gains. Check each product's docs.
  • "Up to" is a ceiling: all three pools are tiered; your actual APR follows the schedule (not fully printed — pending verification #1) plus, for USD1, the 50,000-unit per-user cap on the new/incremental pool.
  • Pool exhaustion. "Subject to availability" means the KGST 1–1.5M pool can fill before 12-11; the USD1 2.5M incremental pool is likewise finite and per-user capped.
  • Liquidity timing. Flexible products redeem on the product's own terms — check the delay if you need funds between distributions.
  • Dates are fixed — and 11-13 is not one. The announcement explicitly schedules no distribution on 11-13 [announcement]; first payout is 11-20.
  • Edition-specific. Windows, pools and product lines are edition-specific — do not copy eligibility assumptions from editions one and two.

FAQ

Is this an airdrop? No — hold-to-earn: you earn by holding/subscribing qualifying assets; rewards distribute on 11-20/11-27/12-04 with no claim page in the announcement.

Which pool is best for me? Already hold USD1 anywhere on Binance? Pool A is the largest upside with no new commitment — the all-holdings portion is uncapped. Want the top headline APR? Pool C reaches 15% but is first-come-first-served. Smallest commitment: Pool B (0.01 USDe, 24h, 5.25% floor).

Do my futures positions count? For USD1, yes — Futures and Funding accounts are in the eligible list [announcement].

What is WLFI doing in the USD1 pool? The pool is 120M WLFI + 7M USD1 — part of the reward is a non-stablecoin token (WLFI), a small exposure inside a stablecoin campaign [announcement; WLFI identity = background, pending verification #2].

When is the first payout? 2026-11-20 — no distribution on 11-13 [announcement].

What if I hold USDe above 100,000? The printed structure covers balances up to 100,000 USDe [announcement]; above that, treatment follows the not-fully-printed schedule (pending verification #1).

Where does it update? The schedule is fixed by the announcement; any Binance amendment gets re-verified against the official text and appended here.
