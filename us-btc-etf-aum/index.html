The Core Concept: AUM Moves for Two Reasons (Flows + Price)

AUM = cumulative net creations (real money delivered in, minus money redeemed out, valued at settlement prices) plus the mark-to-market value of the bitcoin held. So any AUM change decomposes into:

  • Factor 1 — net flows: creations minus redemptions. This is the Farside daily table; the 8-session window 11-02 → 11-11 printed −$1,877.5M (7 outflow sessions, the lone positive day 11-04 at +$74.6).
  • Factor 2 — price revaluation: the same held bitcoin worth more or less. Over the same window BTC fell −8.1% ($77,698.6 → $71,381.2, OKX daily closes) — a pure revaluation effect with zero flow attached.

That is why the complex moved from the $100B region to $93.7B: the −$1.9B of redemptions is the flow half, and the −8.1% price move on roughly a $40B+ revaluation base is the other half. Neither number alone explains the AUM change — and that is also why ETF flows and BTC price do not move 1:1.
