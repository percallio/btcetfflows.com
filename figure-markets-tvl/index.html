What Figure is

Per The Block's 2026-01-14 coverage, Figure launched an onchain equity network aimed at remaking stock trading and lending — i.e., the venue side of tokenized equities: trading markets for the tokens plus a lending layer over them (post/tokenized stocks as collateral). The Figure Prime / "Democratized Prime" product (per Figure's own legal page: "Democratized Prime — General Terms and Conditions, FGRS Pool Addendum") is the prime-brokerage-style pool that carries the second TVL entry. In this site's RWA map, Figure sits with tokenized NVDA on Solana (the single-name flow), EQX tokenized stocks (the Hyperliquid single-stock venue), and Ondo MAG7X (the basket product) — Figure is the network/venue layer those flows run on.
