Three Different Stories in One Window

  • IBIT — the concentrated outflow. Negative on 8 of 8 sessions, −$1,770.6M, 94.3% of the window's market net; the two biggest prints in the complex were IBIT's (11-02 −$405.3M, 11-06 −$403.0M). Any headline reading "bitcoin ETFs bled this week" is, in dollars, an IBIT story — it is the fund institutions were redeeming from. Context on the fund and how to hold it: IBIT main page.
