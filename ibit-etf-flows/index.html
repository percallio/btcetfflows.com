How to Read ETF Flow (Mechanism, Long-Tail)

  • Creations and redemptions are the only tap. Institutional investors (authorized participants) create shares by delivering BTC/USD to the fund and redeem by taking them out. Daily flow = net of the two, valued by Farside at the day's prices.
  • Wallet moves precede, or accompany, settlement. A spot ETF's custodian can move coins to an exchange as the logistics step for redemption settlement or custodial rebalancing — that is what the 2,252 BTC + 72,210 ETH transfer was: onchain movement, not a sell print.
  • Flow ≠ price, but flow + price is information. Outflow days that coincide with price weakness are distribution; outflow days with rising prices are often product rebalancing. This week's shape (the market negative 6 of 7 days into a price drawdown, 11-04 the lone +74.6) is the former — which is why the page refreshes hourly: the 11-11 row lands first here.
  • Watch the IBIT column, not the Total. "BlackRock" in a headline maps to the IBIT column. The Total blends a dozen funds whose flows can be sign-opposite in the same day (see 11-04: market +74.6 while IBIT was −201.2).
