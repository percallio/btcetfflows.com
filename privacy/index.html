Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Short version: this site runs on two kinds of data — anonymous analytics, and what you hand over deliberately (an email, a payment memo, a message). We sell none of it, and we don't cross-site track you.

This page is the whole policy: collection, non-collection, the third-party list, and your rights. If it ever gets longer, the date at the top tells you it changed.

What We Collect

1. Anonymous analysis. Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager. A cookie on this site that records page views, referral source, and device category (mobile/desktop, browser class). It sees this site's pages and nothing else — no cross-site tracking, no audience lists, no data shared with any other product.

2. What you give us, deliberately. Three things, all on your initiative:

  • Subscription email — the weekly digest signup at /digest/ is hosted by Buttondown; the address lives in Buttondown's system, and we use it only to send the digest.
  • Payment memo — paid data subscriptions at /subscribe/ settle in USDT/USDC; the on-chain payment memo carries your email + plan tier, and that pair is what identifies your account. No other payment details are stored.
  • Messages to us — contact/feedback content sent via the Buttondown note field or the footer contact line, kept until we reply and you ask for removal.

What We Don't

  • No selling data — to anyone, for anything, ever under this policy.
  • No third-party ad-vendor cookies. When AdSense is enabled, it uses Google's limited cookies per Google's policy (ad personalization + measurement); we add no other ad stack and no ad network of our own.
  • No cross-site behavioral tracking — analytics stop at this site's pages; we don't follow you anywhere else.
  • Data stays on the rails — analysis runs on Google's infrastructure, email on Buttondown's, the site on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare; we don't move your data between any of them.

Your Rights

Unsubscribe — one click, in the footer of every digest email; the address leaves the list at the next sync.

Refund — paid customers: remaining days pro-rated, returned in USDT to the original chain (TRC20 or EVM, same address you paid from).

Delete your email — contact us with the address (any footer channel or the Buttondown note field); we remove it from every list we manage and confirm.

What Happens When You Visit

  1. Your browser requests a page from GitHub Pages, in front of Cloudflare (CDN + WAF).
  2. Google Tag Manager loads and fires Google Analytics 4 — the only cookie this site sets.
  3. That's all: no fingerprinting scripts, no consent-banner storage, no ad requests until AdSense is enabled (limited cookies per Google policy).

FAQ

Do you use cookies? One — the GA4 cookie delivered via GTM, for anonymous page analytics on this site only.

Where is my digest email stored? In Buttondown's system, not ours; unsubscribe from any email and the address leaves at the next sync.

How does the paid API know who I am? The email in your payment memo — no login, no account store, no other identifier.

Is AdSense a cookie monster? When enabled, AdSense runs on Google's standard policy cookies (ad personalization + measurement) — the same Google infrastructure as GA4, no additional vendors.
