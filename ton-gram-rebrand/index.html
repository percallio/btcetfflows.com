Toncoin to Gram: TON Rebrand to GRAM — Migration Timeline, 1:1 Conversion, and How to Buy GRAM

Written by Crypto Desk · Last verified: 2026-11-12 01:00 UTC

Short answer: Toncoin (TON) has been rebranded to Gram (GRAM). It is the same asset — one token, one supply, one set of wallet addresses — with a new name and a new ticker. Balances were converted at a fixed 1 TON = 1 GRAM ratio on the major exchanges during the migration window, and the ticker change is permanent: any "TON" you see listed today is either legacy data or a different project.

What the Rebrand Actually Changed (and What Did Not)

  • Changed: the name (Toncoin → Gram) and the ticker (TON → GRAM) across exchange products and data platforms.
  • Not changed: the token, the supply, the chain, or your wallet. CoinGecko kept the same coin record — its internal id remains the-open-network — and only the display name/symbol fields were updated to "Gram (prev. Toncoin)" [CoinGecko, 11-12 snapshot]. Wallet address format (ton://…) is unchanged, because an address is a chain property, not a ticker property.
  • Practical consequence: if an exchange, app, or chart still says "TON" today, verify which record it is pointing at before you trust the price. Same name, different era.

How to Buy GRAM Today

  1. Use a GRAM pair, not a TON pair. On Bybit the live pairs are GRAM/USDT, GRAM/USDC and GRAM/EUR (live since Jun 22) [Bybit announcement]. If you see TON/USDT still open somewhere, check the venue's migration notice first.
  2. Standard CEX flow: buy USDT (or use your quote asset) → open the GRAM market → market/limit order → GRAM lands in your spot balance, withdrawable to your own wallet from Jun 22 6:00 UTC onward [Bybit].
  3. To a self-custody wallet: withdraw GRAM using the TON network as before — the address format did not change with the rebrand (see above).
  4. Verify your record: after the first trade, confirm your exchange shows "GRAM" in the asset name. The ticker is the era marker.

Risks and Things to Check

  • Name collisions on both sides. "Gram" is a common word — data platforms now label the coin "Gram (prev. Toncoin)" precisely to avoid confusion [CoinGecko]. Conversely, anything still branded "Toncoin" in a tool you use may be showing the pre-rebrand record.
  • Perpetuals were closed, not renamed. Bybit's TON perps (TONUSDT/TONUSDC/TONUSD inverse) were delisted on Jun 15 with open positions force-closed — they were not migrated to a GRAM-perp product on that schedule [Bybit announcement]. Derivatives exposure had to be settled or moved deliberately.
  • The 1:1 ratio is a conversion mechanic, not a price promise. 1 TON = 1 GRAM means you hold the same asset; it does not pin the dollar price, which has moved since June (see hourly table).
  • Earn products had staggered deadlines. If you sat on TON in Bybit Earn products in mid-June, the subscription windows (Jun 11–15) closed one product at a time; missed the manual-redeem window on Liquidity Mining = system settlement [Bybit announcement].

FAQ

Is TON and GRAM the same coin? Yes. Same token, same supply, same addresses; the rebrand changed the name and ticker. CoinGecko's record is literally unchanged in id (the-open-network) and renamed to "Gram (prev. Toncoin)" [CoinGecko].

Was the conversion really 1:1? On Bybit, yes — automatic, during the Jun 15–22 suspension window, 1 TON = 1 GRAM [Bybit announcement].

Did my wallet address change? No. Addresses are chain-level; only the ticker changed.

Why rebrand to "Gram"? The exchange announcements frame it as a rebrand of Toncoin; the deeper branding rationale is the project's own (this page tracks the exchange-side mechanics, which is where the searchable questions concentrate).

Can I still buy "TON"? On migrated venues the pairs are GRAM/* now; if a TON pair is still trading somewhere, verify it is the same underlying before trading [Bybit schedule].
