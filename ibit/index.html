What Is IBIT? BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust, ETF Flows, and the 2252 BTC Transfer to Coinbase

Written by Crypto Desk · Last verified: 2026-11-12 02:35 UTC (raw pulls 11-12 09:57–10:09 UTC+8, Farside / Odaily / CMC)

IBIT is the iShares Bitcoin Trust — BlackRock's spot Bitcoin ETF, listed on Nasdaq since January 2024 and running at a 0.25% management fee [Farside Investors fee table, pulled 11-12]. It is the vehicle through which a large share of traditional-finance Bitcoin demand enters the market, which is why its daily flows are a first-order indicator for BTC: this week, while the wider US spot ETF complex bled, BlackRock moved 2,252 BTC plus 72,210 ETH (≈$318.36M) from an ETF wallet to Coinbase in roughly 30 minutes [Odaily citing Onchain Lens, 11-10 11:50 UTC; CMC/BSCN headline, 11-12] — a transfer, not a sale, that nonetheless landed on the worst outflow week of the month.

What IBIT Is (The Definition That Survives the News Cycle)

Strip the transfer headlines away and IBIT is a specific, checkable product:

  • A spot Bitcoin ETF. The trust holds actual BTC (and ETH, in the wallet structure Onchain Lens observed) in custody and issues shares that track the asset's value minus fees. It is not a futures product and not a wrapper — the underlying sits in the fund's wallets [Odaily/Onchain Lens wallet-level report, 11-10].
  • Issued and managed by BlackRock (iShares brand), listed on Nasdaq, live since the first US spot Bitcoin ETFs began trading in January 2024 [background: US spot BTC ETF launch window].
  • Fee: 0.25% per year [Farside Investors, fee column, pulled 11-12] — mid-pack in a complex where fees now range from 0.14% to 1.50% (GBTC sits at the top end at 1.50%) [Farside fee row].
  • Why flows matter: spot ETF creations and redemptions move real BTC between the fund's custody wallets and exchanges. A sustained outflow streak removes dry powder from the market structure; an inflow streak adds it. That is why IBIT has a permanent data page on this site (below) — the transfer news is the hook, the flow series is the long-tail asset.

Cumulative picture (Farside, since launch, US$m): IBIT +64,810 — the largest fund in the complex — while the market total is +55,440; FBTC +10,238; MSBT +865; GBTC (28,149); and a Farside-labeled "BTC" fund (0.15% fee) +2,879 [Farside Investors "Total" row, pulled 11-12]. Two reads: IBIT has been a consistent net-inflow fund for two years and now carries more than the complex's own net total (GBTC's −28.1B, the only net-outflow fund since launch, is the main offset); funds still move in opposite directions on the same day — 11-04: market +74.6 while IBIT was −201.2, on the flow data page.

This Week's Headline: 2,252 BTC + 72,210 ETH to Coinbase

The verified facts, both sources:

  • What moved: 2,252 BTC (≈$166.94M) and 72,210 ETH (≈$151.42M), total ≈$318.36M [Odaily, 2026-11-10 19:50 UTC+8 (= 11:50 UTC), citing Onchain Lens monitoring — original figures, not the ~$173M single-asset estimate that circulated earlier].
  • From where: BlackRock's ETF wallet — the custody structure that holds the funds' BTC/ETH.
  • To where: Coinbase [Odaily/Onchain Lens; corroborated by the CMC/BSCN guest-post headline "BlackRock Transfers Largest Single-Day $BTC to Coinbase Prime", published 11-12 — 待核: the "largest single-day" superlative; full text JS-rendered, not re-pulled].
  • How fast: within ~30 minutes [Onchain Lens via Odaily].
  • Implied prices: $166.94M ÷ 2,252 ≈ $74.1K/BTC; $151.42M ÷ 72,210 ≈ $2,097/ETH — consistent with the OKX 11-09 snapshot of $73,714 BTC [OKX Planet, 11-09].

It is a transfer, not a sale. Coins moved to an exchange are eligible to be sold; they have not been. For a spot ETF, wallet-to-exchange movement is the logistics step behind redemptions and custody rebalancing (mechanism: event page).

What to Watch (Tripwires, Not Predictions)

  • IBIT-specific direction, not the market total. The market number blends a dozen funds. The IBIT column is the one that matches "BlackRock" in a headline — this week it was negative all seven days; the "positive on 4 of 7" divergence story belongs to FBTC.
  • The 11-11 row. Farside had the IBIT column pending at pull time (partial: market total −30.1 posted excluding IBIT; MSBT +9.0, GBTC −39.1). It lands on the hourly data page first.
  • Second wallet movement. A repeat of a 30-minute transfer within 48h would upgrade "operational" to "systematic" in the mechanism read [single event so far — Onchain Lens via Odaily].
  • Price level, not the transfer. The transfer happened into a market that CMC's 11-12 top stories headlined as a deepening crypto drawdown [CMC, 11-12]; the OKX 11-09 snapshot shows BTC at $73,714. Flows follow price more often than price follows a single 30-minute transfer.
