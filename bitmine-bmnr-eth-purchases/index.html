The Purchase Program (What It Is)

  • Plan launched June 30, 2025 — BitMine has bought ETH every week since, funded by equity-raise proceeds, not by selling coins [Business Insider].
  • The funding engine is dilution: over FY2026 the company sold 395.1M common shares, raising $13.2B gross [Business Insider — pending verification #2].
  • Cumulative purchases per The CC Press (2026-10-07): $131M / 32,447 ETH as of that article — the earlier, smaller tranche of the program before the pace stepped up.
  • The recent weekly pace: 9,587 ETH per week [Business Insider, 11-11].

The cap is a purchase ceiling, not a stop-announcement: BitMine stays in accumulation mode until it crosses the line [The CC Press]. A single corporate wallet at 5% of circulating ETH out-concentrates most institutional wallets, exchange reserves and protocol treasuries — the MicroStrategy Bitcoin structure, mirrored on ETH [The CC Press].

Hitting the Cap Is a Catalyst (What Changes After)

  • The weekly buy stops. The ~9,587 ETH/week demand line comes off the market — the last corporate marginal buyer of this size flips to flat.
  • Staking becomes the growth engine. Once at 5%, staking rewards are the main source of new ETH: management values current annualized staking rewards at $309M, and Tom Lee projected up to $359M/year if the entire stack is staked (The Block, cited by Business Insider). Already 5,227,037 ETH — over 86% — is staked via outside partners and the in-house MAVAN network.
  • Yield is dollar-denominated and price-elastic. When ETH falls, the dollar value of staking yield falls with it — a risk the 10-K itself flags; the 9.50% preferred dividend is covered by that cash.
  • The story reprices. Until the cap, BMNR is an accumulation story (disclosure every week, NAV grows every week); after it, the equity question is what the company does with a full stack — hold, stake more, or stop. That endgame is undefined [The CC Press].

FAQ

Is the 5% cap regulatory? No — it is self-imposed by Tom Lee, framed as a "hard stop" on concentration and market impact [The CC Press, 2026-10-07].

When does BitMine publish holdings? Weekly — the last verified point is 2026-11-08 (published 11-11). Each new weekly disclosure refreshes the headroom and the countdown on this page.

Can the cap math change? Yes, in both directions: circulating supply drifts up (more ETH mined/staked), and the weekly pace can accelerate or pause. Both inputs are re-verified at each disclosure.

Does BitMine sell ETH? No coins sold in FY2026 — the $6.313B net loss was an unrealized mark [10-K via The BlockBeats; see the FY2026 loss page].
