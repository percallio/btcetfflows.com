Holdings logic — what you actually get

  • Tech basket: the seven major US tech stocks — a single Mag-7-style bet, but on-chain and 24/7.
  • Crypto beta added: IBIT (spot Bitcoin ETF) and ETHA (spot Ethereum ETF) exposure in the same token — i.e. the basket is deliberately not pure equities; it carries BTC and ETH price risk through the ETF wrappers.
  • Implication: MAG7X moves like "mega-cap tech + crypto" in one line. In a risk-off week it has two ways down (tech selloff and crypto drawdown). In a risk-on week it has two ways up.
  • Structure check: verify in Ondo's MAG7X docs the exact rebalancing rules (does the basket reweight? how are ETF positions represented — tokenized IBIT/ETHA or cash equivalents?) and the wrap/unwrap (mint/redeem) terms before treating it as a passive holding.
