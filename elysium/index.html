What Is Elysium? Kinetiq's Hyperliquid L2 Chain Explained (2026)

Last verified: 2026-11-13 (data pulled 2026-11-12 17:49–18:10 UTC; launch-week table from 11-11 16:00 UTC). Prices below change fast — this page is scheduled for hourly refreshes.

By Crypto Desk.

Elysium is a new Layer 2 network built by Kinetiq for the Hyperliquid ecosystem. It went live on mainnet on 9 November 2026 as an Arbitrum Orbit rollup that settles to HyperEVM, with ~100 millisecond block times aimed at high-frequency spot trading, token launches and the fast-moving memecoin meta. There is no separate gas token: every transaction on Elysium is paid in HYPE, the Hyperliquid token, which means bridging HYPE over is the entry ticket to the whole ecosystem.

This page explains what Elysium is, how the chain works, the current state of its token economy, how to get started, and the risks of an ecosystem that is less than a week old.

What Elysium actually is

Elysium is a dedicated L2 in the Hyperliquid family:

  • Builder: Kinetiq, the team behind wrapped-HYPE tooling (its "Kinetiq Wrapped HYPE" token is listed on CoinGecko).
  • Architecture: Arbitrum Orbit rollup, settling to HyperEVM (not HyperCore, the trading layer). Mainnet launched 2026-11-09.
  • Performance: blocks every ~100 ms, which suits HFT-style spot trading, launchpads and meme volume.
  • Gas: HYPE, native, 1:1. No second gas token.
  • Network parameters (for wallet setup): Chain ID 99901, RPC https://rpc.elysium.kinetiq.xyz, explorer https://elysium.kinetiq.xyz, currency symbol HYPE. Official site/docs: elysium.kinetiq.xyz.

Why a new chain? Hyperliquid's own stack is optimized for perps; the L2 gives the ecosystem a high-throughput spot venue where teams can deploy their own DEXes, launchpads and — the current narrative — AI-agent infrastructure.

The MCP-gateway narrative: AI agents trading on Elysium

The loudest story around Elysium so far is the MCP (Model Context Protocol) angle. ELYPORT, one of the first tokens to build a token-profile on the chain, describes itself as "Your AI, docked on Elysium. MCP gateway for Elysium and Hyperliquid: read, simulate, swap, trade. You sign every move." An open-source MCP server (elysium-chain-mcp on GitHub, ElysiumForge-Labs) lets AI agents read and interact with the chain — the agent proposes actions and the human wallet still signs each one.

That is the pitch of the "AgentFi" moment for Hyperliquid-adjacent chains: give trading bots a standard interface, keep human signatures in the loop, and let the token market price the narrative. It is early and speculative — treat it as a theme to track, not a finished product.

How to get started on Elysium (bridge walkthrough)

The route is: CEX → HyperEVM → Elysium. The Elysium bridge only accepts HYPE sitting on HyperEVM, not on HyperCore and not on an exchange.

  1. Buy HYPE on a centralised exchange, then withdraw to your self-custody wallet on the HyperEVM network. Double-check the network on the withdrawal screen — wrong-network sends are hard to recover. (If your HYPE is on HyperCore, first use "Transfer to EVM" inside the Hyperliquid app to move it to HyperEVM at the same address.)
  2. Add Elysium to your wallet (MetaMask with manual network settings, OKX Wallet, Zerion, or the one-click "add network" on the official bridge page): Chain ID 99901, RPC https://rpc.elysium.kinetiq.xyz, symbol HYPE, explorer https://elysium.kinetiq.xyz.
  3. Open the official bridge from the Elysium docs at elysium.kinetiq.xyz — not from search results or social links (several unrelated sites use the name "Elysium").
  4. Set source = HyperEVM, token = HYPE, destination = Elysium. The rate is 1:1; the bridge fee is paid in HYPE on the source side, so keep a small buffer.
  5. Do a small test transfer first, confirm arrival on the explorer, then move the rest.

Once HYPE is on Elysium you can pay gas, swap on the deployed DEXes, join launchpads, and experiment with the AI/MCP tooling — or simply hold through the memecoin wave and see what lasts.

Risks

  • Age. Mainnet is three days old. Contracts, bridges and liquidity have had almost no time to be stress-tested.
  • Name collision. Multiple unrelated projects and domains use "Elysium". Bookmark the official docs; verify URLs before signing.
  • Narrative premium. The MCP/AgentFi story is a theme, not revenue yet. Token profiles built on hype (ELYPORt-class memecoins) can go to zero the way new-chain memes usually do.
  • Thin liquidity. Early pools are small; a modest market order moves the price, and the launch-day +3222% pool artifact above is the warning.
  • Ecosystem beta. Elysium inherits Hyperliquid's price risk: HYPE is both your gas and the gravity well for everything bridged over.
