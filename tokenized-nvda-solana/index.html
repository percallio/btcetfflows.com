What is tokenized NVDA — and what is WNVDAX?

Tokenized NVDA is a family, not one product. The mechanism, as documented 11-12:

  • xStocks is the issuer/standard — per the 11-12 article, the leading tokenization standard for on-chain equities and the largest NVDA tokenized-equity liquidity pool that day.
  • 1:1 structure. The core token (NVDAx) has a supply of 321,802.47 tokens, each corresponding to one NVDA share, held by 207,000+ wallets. One token stands behind one real share — not a derivative, not a fund.
  • WNVDAX = "Wrapped NVIDIA xStock" — the wrapped form of that xStocks token, listed on Bitget (WNVDAX/USDT0), indexed by CoinGecko (wrapped-nvidia-xstock). Multi-chain (underlying on Ethereum/Arbitrum/Mantle, wrapped on xLayer/Monad); dominant 11-12 pool: Uniswap V3 on xLayer ($9.25M volume).
  • How it differs from an ETF: an ETF is a fund — basket, issuer, expense ratio, exchange-listed, market-hours trading. A tokenized stock is a 1:1 on-chain representation of one share — no fund wrapper, cost lives in the wrap/spread, trades 24/7 with off-hours prices set by the on-chain book. FAQ

How do I buy tokenized NVIDIA stock on Solana?

Put SOL or USDC in a Solana wallet and swap for the xStocks NVDA token (NVDAx) on a Solana DEX (Raydium/Meteora class) or aggregator. Verify the contract against the xStocks listing first — 20 markets, $6.9M total liquidity, deepest book >$3M: thin — limit orders, size for slippage. CEX route: WNVDAX on Bitget (WNVDAX/USDT0).

What is WNVDAX?

WNVDAX is "Wrapped NVIDIA xStock" — the wrapped form of the xStocks 1:1 NVIDIA share token, listed on Bitget. One token stands behind one NVDA share (core series supply: 321,802.47 tokens vs the same number of shares). On 2026-11-12 it traded at $165.09, down ~9% in 24h, with $9.14M 24h volume.

Is tokenized stock trading 24/7?

Yes — no market close, no weekends. Off-hours prices are set by the on-chain book, not Nasdaq, so expect drift (opportunity and gap risk). Live example from 11-12: with Nasdaq still at its $173.73 pre-open position, on-chain NVDAx traded at $163.92 — the 24/7 book pricing the resignation before the opening bell.

Did the Jensen Huang resignation change tokenized NVDA volume?

Yes, on 11-12: Solana's 24h tokenized NVDA spot volume ran $84M to an $88M peak — more than all other chains combined (dual source); WNVDAX did $9.14M (Bitget) / $10.84M (CoinGecko aggregate) in 24h; HIP-3 NVDA perps did $61M+ with $128.5M OI.
