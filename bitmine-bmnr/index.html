What Is BitMine (BMNR)? The ETH Treasury Company Behind the $6.313B FY2026 Net Loss

Written by Crypto Desk · Last verified: 2026-11-12 05:15 UTC

BitMine Immersion Technologies (NYSE: BMNR per Yahoo Finance market data) is the largest corporate Ethereum holder in the world: as of November 8, 2026 it held 6,070,092 ETH — 4.97% of the ~122.2M circulating supply (BitMine holdings update of Nov 9, relayed by The BlockBeats, 2026-11-11 01:10:37 UTC, and confirmed by Business Insider, 2026-11-11 18:10:07 UTC). The company is now in the news because its 10-K for the fiscal year ended August 31, 2026 reported a net loss of $6.313 billion (The BlockBeats relay of the filing; The Block and Business Insider round the same figure to $6.3B, and OKX Orbit's FY analysis rounds it to $6.32B) — driven almost entirely by ~$6.229B of unrealized losses on its ETH stack as ether fell roughly 44% across that fiscal year.

What BitMine Actually Is

In one sentence: BitMine is a public company whose business is buying, holding, and staking Ethereum — the MicroStrategy playbook, executed on ETH.

  • Treasury company, not a DeFi protocol. It buys ETH with equity-raise proceeds, stakes most of it, books price moves through earnings, and sells stock — not coins — to keep buying; every week since the plan launched June 30, 2025 [BI].
  • The stack is staked. 5,227,037 ETH — over 86% — via outside partners and the in-house MAVAN network (launched March 2026 after buying Australia's Pier Two) [The BlockBeats; BI].
  • Revenue is staking revenue. FY2026 revenue $124.8M (BlockBeats: $125M), ~$121.4M of it staking/validation = 97%, up from $6.1M a year earlier [dual-source].
  • Not leveraged. Liabilities $33.7M vs ~$15.43B assets = 0.22% — no borrowing to buy ETH [OKX Orbit, 11-12 — pending verification #2].
  • Scale rank. Second-largest public crypto treasury behind Strategy (MSTR) [BI]; chairman Tom Lee's public ceiling: stop buying at 5% of supply [The CC Press 10-07; BI 11-11].

How the 5% ETH Supply Cap Works (The Mechanism)

This is the part of the story that holds up after the news cycle ends — it defines what BitMine will and will not do for the rest of the accumulation phase:

  • The rule. Tom Lee: BitMine will halt ETH purchases once holdings reach 5% of the circulating supply — self-imposed, not regulatory, framed as a "hard stop" on concentration and market impact [The CC Press, 2026-10-07].
  • The math. ~122.2M circulating ETH × 5% ≈ 6.11M ETH ceiling. Holdings on Nov 8 were 6,070,092, leaving roughly 40,000 ETH to buy — at last week's pace of 9,587 ETH per week, the buying spree ends in about four weeks [Business Insider; supply figure dual-confirmed by BI and The CC Press].
  • A limit, not a stop. This is a purchase ceiling, not an announcement that buying has stopped: BitMine is still in accumulation mode until it crosses the line [The CC Press].
  • Life after the cap. Once the 5% line is hit, staking rewards become the main source of new ETH. Management values current annualized staking rewards at $309M, and Lee projected up to $359M/year if the entire stack is staked (The Block, cited by Business Insider). But the dollar value of that yield falls when ETH falls — a risk the 10-K itself flags.
  • Why it matters. A single corporate wallet at 5% of circulating ETH out-concentrates most institutional wallets, exchange reserves, and protocol treasuries — the MicroStrategy Bitcoin structure, mirrored on ETH [The CC Press].

Risk Checklist (What Actually Can Hurt You)

  • ETH price beta, booked quarterly. The $6.313B loss shows the mechanism's worst direction: the mark swings through net income every period, with no coins sold.
  • Dilution is the funding engine. BitMine sold 395.1M common shares, raising $13.2B gross, over the fiscal year [pending verification #2]; holdings growth is purchased with share-count growth.
  • Staking yield in dollars shrinks as ETH falls, even if the ETH earned is unchanged [10-K warning via BI].
  • Reward-rate compression squeezes the cash covering the 9.50% preferred dividend as more of the network stakes [10-K via BI].
  • The cap changes the story. At ~5% of supply the buying ends; the post-cap endgame (hold / stake more / done) is undefined [The CC Press].

FAQ

Is the $6.313B loss real money gone? No — an unrealized mark; zero coins sold, the loss is ETH's ~44% FY price fall [The BlockBeats; BI].

What exactly is 4.97%? 6,070,092 ETH ÷ ~122.2M circulating (Nov 8) — 0.03 points short of the 5% ceiling [dual-source].

Does BitMine keep buying? Until the cap: ~40,000 ETH left ≈ four weeks at 9,587 ETH/week [BI].

Is it leveraged? Liabilities = 0.22% of total assets per OKX Orbit — no meaningful debt [pending verification #2].

How is it different from August Digital? One is a stock whose NAV is an ETH position; the other a non-custodial USDC vault curator on Monad — compare page.
