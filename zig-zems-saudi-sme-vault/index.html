What zEMS Is, and Who Does What

The zEMS Vault is a single onchain product with five named roles — a tokenized private-credit fund in the RWA sense:

  • ZIG Finance — issuer/platform; zEMS is the first product of its strategic partnership with Laser Digital, with a stated plan to expand the model to other emerging markets and credit strategies.
  • Laser Digital (Nomura) — risk framework advisor. (Four sources say Nomura; one headline says Mizuho — see the discrepancy note below.)
  • Beehive — the lending infrastructure that originates and services the underlying Saudi SME loan portfolio.
  • KAIO — tokenization of the loan portfolio and full lifecycle management of the vault.
  • Zamanat — investment-structure design and Sharia-compliance advisory.

Mechanics: deployed on BNB Chain; investors subscribe and redeem in stablecoins; KYC at both the subscription and redemption stages; open to eligible institutional investors, not retail. The underlying is a diversified pool of Saudi SME private credit — loans made outside the public bond market — which is the entire point: global institutions previously had few direct routes into those loans, because most are made by local banks and lending platforms.

Why tokenized SME credit exists at all (the long-tail context, all figures HackerNoon 11-12): private credit is a $2T+ market that Moody's projects near $4T by 2030; it makes up ~44% of the ~$51B tokenized real-world-asset market (≈$22B on public blockchains) and overtook tokenized US Treasuries (30% share) in 2026; onchain private credit grew ~180% in the year to early 2026; and onchain private-credit products have typically offered 8–12% yields versus 4–5% for tokenized Treasuries. zEMS is one instance of that flow, pointed at the Gulf.

The Saudi SME Market It Targets

Saudi lending to SMEs reached SR468B ≈ $124.6B at end-2025, up 33% in a year. Vision 2030 aims to lift SMEs' share of total credit from 11.5% to 20% — so the pool a vault like this can draw from is structurally growing, and the $100M raise is a small slice of a two-order-of-magnitude larger book. Whether the vault reaches its $100M target is the number to watch (not yet verifiable — see below).
