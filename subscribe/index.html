How payment works (4 steps)

  1. Choose a plan — Starter / Pro / Full.
  2. Transfer — send exactly the plan price in USDT (or USDC on EVM/Solana) to the address of your chosen chain, and write your email + plan in the memo/remark field (e.g. you@example.com pro). No memo = we can't match the payment to you.
  3. Confirmation email (1–24h) — a background daemon watches the receiving wallets every 30 minutes and flags new on-chain payments; we verify amount + memo by hand and email you the confirmation.
  4. Key + alerts — you receive your personal API key by email, and your email is added to the alert list. Full plan: your dashboard is generated at /dash/yourkey.html on the next hourly refresh.

Monthly billing, refund anytime: we refund pro-rated by the remaining days of your cycle, paid in USDT to the address you paid from (same network). One email is enough — include your API key or paying address.
