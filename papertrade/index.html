Here's what Papertrade is, how the mechanism works, what the numbers show, and where the risks sit.

What is Papertrade?

  • Category: Derivatives (DefiLlama), running as an "Off Chain" category on DefiLlama with its token side on Hyperliquid L1 / HyperEVM — i.e. it is part of the Hyperliquid stack, the same infrastructure family as the spot builder DEXes.
  • Product: a derivatives terminal where positions execute with a fixed-rate discount locked in at execution (the mechanism DefiLlama records for the protocol's fees). In practice: you get a deterministic, pre-agreed execution rate at order time instead of chasing a moving book.
  • The new "terminal": trade on Papertrade with the same tooling the treasury uses — one-click trading, take-profits and stop-losses that run without you watching, and delta-neutral pairs and "engines" (docs: docs.paperstrategy.xyz).

The PAPER / PSTR mechanism (the unusual part)

From PaperStrategy's own documentation:

  1. PAPER cannot be bought. Papertrade's PAPER token is minted only from trading losses — there is no pool to buy from and no way to deposit into an LP. The only way to acquire PAPER is to lose money on Papertrade, deliberately or not.
  2. Staked PAPER earns protocol revenue in USDC. It is an income-producing asset most people will never hold, because acquiring it takes capital, time and method.
  3. PSTR is the tradeable proxy. PSTR (PaperStrategy) is a token on HyperEVM built on top of Papertrade. Every buy and sell pays a 10% trade tax: one tenth goes to the team (paid in HYPE), the rest funds a treasury. The treasury runs Papertrade accounts that lose USDC in a controlled way to acquire PAPER at the lowest possible cost, then stake all of it. The staking yield is used to buy PSTR back on the market and burn it.
  4. The loop: the more PSTR trades → the more PAPER the treasury holds → the more yield → the more PSTR gets burned. Supply is 1,000,000,000 PSTR, all in the pool at launch — no presale, no team tokens, no airdrop.
  5. Launch window mechanics: the tax opens at 99% and drops one point per minute to 10% after 89 minutes — a designed incentive to trade early. Liquidity is held by a timelock requiring a 30-day public notice to withdraw; the team runs a V1 multisig with on-chain public actions.

What PSTR is not: it is not a share of the treasury, it gives no direct claim on the treasury's PAPER or its yield — the only link is the buyback-burn.

How to use Papertrade

  1. Docs first: docs.paperstrategy.xyz — terminal setup, "orders, pairs and engines", fees and safety.
  2. Fund with USDC/HYPE in a Hyperliquid-compatible wallet (HyperEVM side).
  3. Trade the terminal: one-click positions, TP/SL that persist, delta-neutral pairs.
  4. If you're playing the token loop: buy PSTR (10% tax applies to you too), understand you are funding the loss-engine, and that your return path is the burn — not a dividend.
  5. Track the fees here and on DefiLlama; a fee spike is the leading indicator that the loop is working (or that activity is concentrating).

Risks

  • Off-chain accounting. DefiLlama classifies the protocol "Off Chain" and flags that the exact fee breakdown isn't available on-chain — trust in the reported numbers is higher than in a fully transparent vault.
  • The loss-engine is contrarian. The treasury's edge is controlled losing. If the strategy that mints PAPER underperforms, the buyback pressure on PSTR weakens while the 10% tax keeps taking.
  • Tax on both sides. 10% on buys and sells is a large cost for active traders; it is only "fair" if the burn loop delivers.
  • Concentration risk. Fees +779% in 7 days can mean one or a few large players, not a broad user base — check the holder/tx concentration before extrapolating.
  • Ecosystem beta. PSTR lives on HyperEVM; HYPE drawdowns drag the whole stack.
