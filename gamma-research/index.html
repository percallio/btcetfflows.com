How to Deposit (Short Version — per the vault listing, UI not pulled this round)

  1. You need the underlying: the main vault takes AUSD (the stablecoin it manages; issuer not named in either source — pending verification #2). A USDC-denominated vault (earnAUSD Loop) and an Aptos USDC vault also exist in the list [TradingStrategy].
  2. Deposit through the hosting platform, Upshift, on the Gamma/earnAUSD vault page [TradingStrategy: all six vaults are Upshift-hosted]. Expect the 3-day deposit delay listed for the main vault [TradingStrategy fees/latency column].
  3. You receive the receipt token earnAUSD, which tracks the vault's AUSD value net of strategy performance.
  4. Yield expectation: the main vault printed 7.0% 1M annualized / 6.8% 3M annualized / 5.2% lifetime absolute [TradingStrategy, single source — pending verification #1]; fees are 0.0% management / 10.0% performance [TradingStrategy].
  5. Optional extra layer (Gamma's 10-22 X post): deposit the earnAUSD receipt into the Morpho earnAUSD/USDC market on Monad for ~2% extra via Merkl incentives ("total yield ~10% APY, no borrowing required") — an on-chain step, not part of the vault [Gamma X].
  6. Exit: redemption follows the hosting platform's flow; the listed 3d deposit delay is the liquidity window to check before sizing a position [TradingStrategy].

Risks (Curator-Specific)

  • Curation is a moving target. Curators rotate strategies with the market. And "may have more vaults outside supported blockchains" means the visible six are a floor, not the whole book [TradingStrategy].
  • Protocol technical risk rated "Severe" by the data source. All six vaults carry TradingStrategy's Severe technical-risk tag — their metric, not ours: the underlying stack (Upshift/vault contracts) is flagged, independent of strategy quality [TradingStrategy].
  • Fee drag is performance-linked. 10.0% performance fee on the main vault [TradingStrategy] — in a down month you pay nothing extra, in a good month a tenth of the gain goes to the curator.
  • Liquidity timing. 3-day deposit delay on the main vault [TradingStrategy]; an urgent exit is a 3-day conversation, not a swap.
  • Concentration. ~99% of the visible $26.0M sits in the single Monad earnAUSD vault ($21.73M of $26.0M) [TradingStrategy] — the "six vaults" story is really "one vault plus five small ones."
  • Precedent drawdowns, two scales. The main vault: $31.5M → $17.2M (−45%) over September→October [DefiLlama history]. An older Upshift USDC vault in the list: peak $17.17M → current $552K (−96.8%) [TradingStrategy] — that one looks like capital migration/closure rather than loss — vaults grow, vaults drain.
  • APY is single-sourced this round. DefiLlama does not publish APY for this curator record; all yield figures are TradingStrategy's [pending verification #1].
